# Balance notes - initial test values

These are starting values, not final design promises.

- Air capacity: 5.0 seconds.
- Rise speed: 270 px/s.
- Small air bubble: +1.5 seconds.
- Rescue window after air reaches zero: 3.5 seconds.
- Fall gravity: 1180 px/s2.
- Max fall speed: 760 px/s.
- Rescue balloon placement: 560-820 px below Milo.
- Revive count: 1 per run.
- Revive air: 100 percent.
- Revive protection: 2.0 seconds.
- Pixels per meter: 12.

Phone testing should answer:

1. Is five seconds of base air too generous or too strict?
2. Can a new player understand the low-air warning before the first fall?
3. Is 3.5 seconds exciting without feeling unfair?
4. Is the rescue balloon visible early enough to plan a route?
5. Can Milo move laterally enough while falling to reach it?
6. Are obstacle gaps readable at phone size?
7. Does the revive feel like a second chance rather than a requirement?
